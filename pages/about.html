About Virgo Plus

Trusted importer of industrial welding and cutting solutions

Leading Welding Equipment Manufacturer

Virgo Solutions India LLP  is engaged in the manufacturing and importing of welding and cutting equipment under the VIRGO PLUS brand.

Virgo Plus provides high-quality welding and cutting equipment. Established with a vision to deliver world-class welding solutions to Indian industries, we have grown into a trusted name in the welding equipment sector through perseverance, reliability, and customer-focused service.

Our management has always been committed to providing equipment that meets the highest standards of quality and performance for its customers. We work with strong and trusted partners, efficient systems, and a reliable team to uphold the VIRGO commitment through prompt support and access to the latest welding technologies.

Our products are designed to be  affordable, heavy-duty, durable, and reliable , helping customers achieve superior performance and productivity.

VIRGO PLUS offers a comprehensive range of welding and cutting solutions, including ARC Welding, TIG Welding, TIG AC/DC Welding, MIG Welding, MIG Pulse Welding, Submerged Arc Welding, Plasma Cutting, Stud Welding, and Laser Welding & Cutting Equipment . Each product is engineered for dependable performance and undergoes quality checks to ensure reliability and customer satisfaction.
