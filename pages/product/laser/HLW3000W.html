Product Overview

The Virgo Plus HLW 3000W Hand Laser Welding Machine delivers 3000W of high-performance fiber laser power with excellent beam quality (BPP) that remains constant over the full power range. A small spot can still be obtained with a long focal length, making it ideal for precision welding applications across a wide range of metal materials including stainless steel, iron, aluminum, and galvanized plate.

Powered by a MAX Photonics 3000W laser source with a pump source life exceeding 100 thousand hours, the HLW 3000W offers maintenance-free operation, electro-optical conversion efficiency of over 25%, and a small, easy-to-install design. The Raytools 120° ergonomic hand solder joint ensures operator comfort and control, while the dual cooling water system and 24-hour continuous operation capability make it suitable for demanding industrial environments.
